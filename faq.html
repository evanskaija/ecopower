Using Makange

Makange can burn for several hours depending on the stove, airflow and cooking load.
There may be a little smoke while lighting. Once lit, Makange is designed to burn cleanly and steadily.
Add a starter and dry sticks to a level stove, light the sticks, then add Makange while leaving gaps for airflow.
Makange is made from recovered charcoal dust, agricultural waste and forest waste.
