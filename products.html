Lighting guide

How to light Makange

Four simple steps to get a steady cooking fire.

1. Set up the stove

Place the stove on firm, level ground with the air door open.

2. Add a starter

Lay a first layer of Makange and add a few dry sticks in the centre.

3. Light the briquettes

Cover the sticks with more Makange, leaving gaps for air, then light the sticks.

4. Ready to cook

When the briquettes glow red, put your pot on and cook.
