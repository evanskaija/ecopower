About Us

Cooking Energy for a Changing World

EcoPower Solutions Ltd is a Tanzanian owned clean energy company with a mission to make safe cooking energy accessible and affordable for everyone. We specialize in biomass briquettes and improved cookstoves designed for households, small vendors, schools, restaurants, and institutions.

Our Mission
To replace harmful and expensive cooking fuels with sustainable solutions that save money, protect the environment, and improve health for communities across Tanzania.

Our Vision
Clean energy for every Tanzanian home. No smoke. No deforestation. No waste.
